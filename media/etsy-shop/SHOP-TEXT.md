## About: story
Houscaper started as a tool for designing miniature houses: pick a style, and it draws every wall, roof and floor as flat parts that slot together. This shop shares the easiest versions of those designs as ready-to-cut files.

Each file is tested so no two parts overlap, every tab has a matching slot, and the parts are numbered in the order you build them. The guide walks you from the base to the roof with a picture for every step, so a first-time builder can finish a house in an afternoon.

The sheets fit an 11 × 19.5 in (279 × 495 mm) bed, which covers Glowforge, xTool and most CO2 and diode lasers. Red lines cut, blue lines engrave.

Want a house that isn't here? You can design your own at houscaper.xyz.

## Message to buyers of digital items
Thank you for your order! Your ZIP has a 3mm and a 2mm folder. Cut the fit-test coupon first and adjust your kerf until a tab slides in snugly, then cut the sheets and follow the PDF guide step by step. If anything doesn't fit, send me a message with a photo and I'll help.
